THE DETAILS THAT MATTER

Your questions.
Straight answers.

Is MoneyPilot legitimate?

MoneyPilot is a technology platform for discovering potential claims and getting help with the process. You can read its service terms and customer feedback, including reviews on Trustpilot. It is not a law firm or a government agency.

Is it free to check my eligibility?

Yes. The eligibility check is free. MoneyPilot offers optional paid plans for assistance. Review the price, included services and billing terms before choosing a plan.

Does a match mean I will get paid?

A match means a claim may be relevant to you. You still need to meet its requirements. Any payment depends on a valid claim, the case outcome and the settlement terms.

Can I do this myself?

Yes. You can review the official information and file an eligible settlement claim yourself. MoneyPilot provides a way to discover potential opportunities and choose help with the process.

Who sends the settlement payment?

For court-approved settlements, the settlement administrator handles eligibility and payments under the settlement terms. MoneyPilot does not decide who qualifies or guarantee a payout.

How long will it take to receive money?

Timing depends on the case, claim review and required approvals. A payment date or amount may not be known when you check or file a claim.
